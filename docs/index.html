Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

HalKhata is built as an open-source, commercial app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

  1. 1. Data Collection and Use

    We respect your privacy. HalKhata does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, phone contacts, business transactions, or financial data to external servers. The app operates 100% offline. All digital ledger entries you record are processed and stored strictly on your own device's secure local storage.

  2. 2. Account Deletion

    Because HalKhata does not create user accounts, require logins, or store information on clouds or external databases, there is no user data or account to delete from our end. Uninstalling the app from your device permanently deletes all local ledger data.

  3. 3. Device Permissions

    The app may request local storage or backup permissions solely to save your ledger data safely on your phone. This data is never shared with the developer or any third party.

  4. 4. Changes to This Policy

    We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

  5. 5. Contact Us

    For app support, feature suggestions, or questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: anikdeveloper0@gmail.com
